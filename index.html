Our story

A Local Name You Can Trust

Poda Barber's is a well-trusted, traditional barbershop right here in Hove, East Sussex — formerly known to many locals as H's Barbershop. Over the years we've built a strong reputation the old-fashioned way: by turning up every day and doing great work at a fair price.

Known for honest pricing, reliability and high-quality haircuts, our experienced team responds quickly to whatever you need. Whether it's a sharp fade before a night out, a tidy beard trim, or a proper hot towel shave, you're in good hands.
