Our Story

Why Poda? Why Us.

Poda Barbers is a well-trusted, traditional barbershop right here in Hove, East Sussex — known to many locals as H's Barbershop before we became Poda. Over the years we've built our reputation the old-fashioned way: by turning up every day and doing great work at a fair price.

"Honest pricing, reliability and high-quality haircuts — that's the whole job, and we take it seriously."

Whether it's a sharp fade before a night out, a tidy beard trim, or a proper hot towel shave, our experienced team responds quickly and looks after you from the moment you sit down. If you need a premium, well-priced haircut near you, come take a seat.
